# Returns and Warranty Policy

Document reference: HCS-RWP-2025
Owner: Halden Cycle Supply B.V., Customer Operations
Version: 3.0, effective 1 February 2025
Supersedes: HCS-RWP-2022 version 2.4

## 1. Scope

This policy covers goods sold by Halden Cycle Supply to trade customers
(bicycle shops, workshops and assemblers). It also defines what the Distributor
recovers from suppliers under clause 7 of the relevant framework agreement.
It does not cover direct-to-consumer sales; Halden Cycle Supply does not sell
to consumers.

## 2. Warranty Periods

Warranty runs from the date of the Distributor's invoice to the trade customer.

| Product category | SKU prefix | Warranty period |
|---|---|---|
| Aluminium frame sets | FRM-ALU | 24 months |
| Carbon frame sets | FRM-CRB | 36 months |
| Forks | FRK-* | 24 months |
| Wheelsets | WHL-* | 24 months |
| Drivetrain components | DRV-* | 12 months |
| Consumables and wear parts | CNS-*, WER-* | No warranty beyond conformity at delivery |

Warranty is limited to defects in materials and workmanship. It is not
transferable beyond the first trade customer and does not extend on
replacement: a unit replaced under warranty carries the remainder of the
original period, not a fresh one.

## 3. Exclusions

The warranty does not cover:

- normal wear, including bearing wear, brake pad wear and tyre wear;
- damage from crash, misuse, or use outside the stated intended discipline;
- corrosion caused by storage in salt-laden or unventilated conditions;
- units where the frame serial number has been altered or removed;
- assembly faults, including overtightened bolts where the applied torque
  exceeded the printed specification;
- paint and decal cosmetic issues reported more than 30 days after delivery.

## 4. Commercial Returns

A commercial return is a return of unused, resaleable stock for reasons other
than a defect.

4.1 Commercial returns must be requested within 30 calendar days of delivery.

4.2 A restocking fee of 15 percent of the invoice value applies to all
commercial returns.

4.3 Goods must be returned in original packaging, unmounted and unmarked. A
frame that has had a headset pressed in is not resaleable and will be refused.

4.4 Special-order and custom-painted items are not eligible for commercial
return under any circumstances.

## 5. RMA Process

5.1 The customer requests an RMA number through the trade portal, giving the
invoice number, SKU code, serial number where applicable, and photographs of
the fault.

5.2 Customer Operations issues or refuses the RMA number within 3 business
days. No return is accepted at the warehouse without an RMA number physically
marked on the outer packaging.

5.3 The customer ships the goods within 14 calendar days of RMA issue. An RMA
number expires after 14 days and must be re-requested.

5.4 Inbound freight for a valid warranty claim is paid by the Distributor.
Inbound freight for a commercial return is paid by the customer.

5.5 On receipt, the goods are inspected within 5 business days against the
Quality Inspection Procedure, document HCS-QIP-2023.

## 6. Resolution

6.1 A valid warranty claim is resolved by repair, replacement, or credit note,
at the Distributor's option, in that order of preference.

6.2 Where no equivalent replacement is available within 30 calendar days, the
customer may require a credit note for the full invoice value.

6.3 A rejected claim is returned to the customer at the customer's cost, with a
written inspection report. The customer may appeal once, in writing, within 15
business days.

## 7. Supplier Recovery

7.1 Every accepted warranty claim is logged against the originating supplier
using the lot number recorded at incoming inspection.

7.2 The Distributor invoices the supplier for the wholesale value plus the
handling fee stated in the framework agreement.

7.3 A supplier whose warranty claim rate exceeds 1.5 percent of units delivered
in any rolling 12-month period is placed under a quality improvement plan and
moved to 100 percent incoming inspection at its own cost.

7.4 Warranty claim rates are reported to suppliers in the same monthly report
as delivery performance.
