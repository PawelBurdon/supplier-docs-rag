# Delivery Service Level Agreement (WITHDRAWN)

Document reference: HCS-SLA-2022
Version: 1.9, issued 3 February 2022
Status: **WITHDRAWN on 14 January 2024.** Superseded by HCS-SLA-2024 version 2.1.
Retained in the document library for reference only, because purchase orders
placed before 14 January 2024 were performed under these terms and a small
number of warranty and penalty disputes from that period remain open.

**Do not quote any figure from this document as current.** Every commercial
term below has been replaced. Where a reader needs today's lead times, penalty
rates, targets or escalation path, the governing document is HCS-SLA-2024.

## 1. Purpose

This SLA defined the lead times, on-time delivery targets, penalties and
escalation path for purchase orders issued by Halden Cycle Supply between
February 2022 and January 2024.

## 2. Supplier Tiers

Two tiers only. The three-tier model, and the probationary tier used for new
suppliers, were introduced with version 2.1.

- Tier A — contracted suppliers with committed capacity.
- Tier B — all other suppliers.

## 3. Committed Lead Times

Lead time was measured in calendar days from purchase order transmission, not
from acknowledgement. The change to acknowledgement, and the two business day
acknowledgement window, came with version 2.1.

| Product category | SKU prefix | Tier A | Tier B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aluminium frame sets | FRM-ALU | 25 days | 50 days |
| Carbon frame sets | FRM-CRB | 32 days | 65 days |
| Forks | FRK-ALU, FRK-CRB | 25 days | 50 days |
| Wheelsets | WHL-* | 22 days | 45 days |
| Drivetrain components | DRV-* | 18 days | 40 days |
| Consumables and wear parts | CNS-*, WER-* | 12 days | 25 days |

Expedited orders were surcharged at 12 percent of order value.

## 4. On-Time Delivery Target

4.1 On-time in-full was measured per supplier per calendar quarter. A delivery
counted as on time if it arrived no later than the committed date and no more
than 5 calendar days early.

4.2 The OTIF target was 94 percent for Tier A and 88 percent for Tier B.

4.3 Missing the target in a quarter triggered a written performance notice.

## 5. Late Delivery Penalties

5.1 A late delivery incurred a penalty of 0.25 percent of the order value for
each calendar day beyond the committed delivery date.

5.2 The penalty was capped at 5 percent of the order value for any single
purchase order.

5.3 A grace period of 2 calendar days applied before the penalty began to
accrue. The grace period was removed in version 2.1.

5.4 Penalties were notified in writing within 45 days of the late delivery.

## 6. Escalation

| Level | Trigger | Contact |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Any delivery forecast to be late | Category buyer |
| 2 | Delivery more than 10 days late | Head of Procurement |
| 3 | OTIF below target for two consecutive quarters | Executive review |

The four-level path, the named response times and the Supply Chain Manager role
were all introduced with version 2.1.

## 7. Force Majeure

Force majeure covered natural disasters, port closures, government action, and
raw material shortages. The exclusion of raw material price movements and of
ordinary subcontractor default was added in version 2.1, after a dispute in
2023 in which a supplier claimed an aluminium price rise as force majeure.

## 8. Measurement and Reporting

Delivery performance was measured from the Distributor's warehouse receipt
records. Suppliers received a quarterly performance report. The move to monthly
reporting came with version 2.1.
