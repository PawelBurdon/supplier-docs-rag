# Delivery Service Level Agreement

Document reference: HCS-SLA-2024
Owner: Halden Cycle Supply B.V., Supply Chain Operations
Version: 2.1, issued 14 January 2024
Applies to: all suppliers operating under a Halden Cycle Supply framework agreement

## 1. Purpose

This SLA defines the lead times, on-time delivery targets, penalties and
escalation path that apply to every purchase order issued by Halden Cycle
Supply. It is incorporated by reference into each framework agreement and
prevails over the framework agreement where the two conflict on lead times or
penalties.

## 2. Supplier Tiers

Suppliers are assigned a tier at onboarding. The tier determines committed lead
times and the on-time delivery target.

- Tier 1 — strategic suppliers, integrated forecasting, consignment stock held
  in Rotterdam.
- Tier 2 — contracted suppliers with committed capacity and a rolling 12-week
  forecast.
- Tier 3 — probationary or low-volume suppliers, no committed capacity.

A supplier's tier is recorded in its onboarding record and is reviewed
annually.

## 3. Committed Lead Times

Lead time is measured in calendar days from purchase order acknowledgement to
arrival at the Rotterdam warehouse. Acknowledgement is due within 2 business
days of order receipt; an unacknowledged order starts its lead time clock on
the third business day regardless.

| Product category | SKU prefix | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aluminium frame sets | FRM-ALU | 21 days | 28 days | 45 days |
| Carbon frame sets | FRM-CRB | 28 days | 35 days | 60 days |
| Forks | FRK-ALU, FRK-CRB | 21 days | 28 days | 45 days |
| Wheelsets | WHL-* | 18 days | 25 days | 40 days |
| Drivetrain components | DRV-* | 14 days | 21 days | 35 days |
| Consumables and wear parts | CNS-*, WER-* | 10 days | 14 days | 21 days |

Expedited orders may be agreed case by case at a surcharge of 8 percent of
order value. An expedite request does not change the committed lead time for
any other open order.

## 4. On-Time Delivery Target

4.1 On-time in-full (OTIF) is measured per supplier per calendar quarter. A
delivery counts as on time if it arrives no later than the committed date and
no earlier than 3 calendar days before it. A delivery counts as in full if the
delivered quantity is within minus 0 and plus 2 percent of the ordered
quantity.

4.2 The OTIF target is 96 percent for Tier 1 and Tier 2 suppliers, and 90
percent for Tier 3 suppliers.

4.3 Missing the OTIF target in a quarter triggers a written performance notice
and a joint root-cause review within 15 business days.

## 5. Late Delivery Penalties

5.1 A late delivery incurs a penalty of 0.5 percent of the order value for each
calendar day beyond the committed delivery date.

5.2 The penalty is capped at 10 percent of the order value for any single
purchase order.

5.3 The penalty accrues from the first late day. There is no grace period.

5.4 Penalties are notified in writing within 30 days of the late delivery and
are settled by set-off against the next payment run.

5.5 Penalties do not apply where the delay is caused by a documented force
majeure event under clause 7, or by the Distributor's own failure to provide
artwork, decals or customer-specific configuration data within the agreed
window.

## 6. Escalation

| Level | Trigger | Contact | Response time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Any delivery forecast to be 1-5 days late | Category buyer | 1 business day |
| 2 | Delivery more than 5 days late, or second late delivery in a quarter | Supply Chain Manager | 1 business day |
| 3 | OTIF below target for two consecutive quarters | Head of Procurement | 5 business days |
| 4 | OTIF below target for three consecutive quarters | Executive review, contract at risk | 10 business days |

Suppliers must notify a foreseeable delay as soon as it is known, and in any
case at least 5 calendar days before the committed delivery date. Late
notification moves the case straight to level 2.

## 7. Force Majeure

Force majeure covers natural disasters, port closures, and government action
that directly prevents production or shipment. It does not cover raw material
price movements, ordinary labour shortages, or a supplier's own subcontractor
default, unless that subcontractor is itself affected by a qualifying event and
the supplier provides evidence within 10 business days.

## 8. Measurement and Reporting

Delivery performance is measured from the Distributor's warehouse receipt
records. Suppliers receive a monthly performance report by the 10th working day
of the following month, showing OTIF, average lateness in days, and penalties
accrued. A supplier may dispute a data point within 15 business days; after
that the figures are final.
