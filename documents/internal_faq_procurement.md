# Procurement Internal FAQ

Maintained by: Halden Cycle Supply B.V., Procurement team
Last edited: 3 June 2025 by R. Okonkwo
Audience: internal only, category buyers and customer operations

This page is written by the buying team for the buying team. It is a working
summary, not a contract. Where it disagrees with a signed framework agreement,
the Delivery SLA or the Returns and Warranty Policy, those documents win. It is
kept on the internal wiki and is edited by anyone in the team.

## Payments

**When do we actually pay suppliers?**
Net 45 from a valid invoice for everyone except Pedalworks, who are on net 30.
"Valid" is doing real work in that sentence: no purchase order number, no
delivery note number, or SKU codes that do not match Schedule A, and finance
bounces it. That happens more often than you would think and the clock restarts
from resubmission, so it is worth checking before the invoice reaches finance.

**Do we take the early payment discount?**
Yes where the supplier offers one and cash allows. Velocore offers 2 percent
for payment within 10 days. On a EUR 60,000 invoice that is EUR 1,200 for
paying 35 days early, which finance considers a good rate.

## Late deliveries

**What do we charge a supplier for a late delivery?**
1 percent of the order value per day late, capped at 15 percent of the order.
Send the penalty notice before the window closes, see below, and it comes off
the next payment run as a set-off.

**Is there a grace period?**
No. It runs from day one late.

**How long do we have to raise the penalty notice?**
60 days from the late delivery. Finance will not process a set-off after that,
so get the notice out while the delivery is still fresh in everyone's memory.

**What if the supplier warned us in advance?**
The penalty still applies. Advance warning changes the escalation path, not the
money. Notification later than 5 days before the committed date pushes the case
straight to escalation level 2.

## Returns

**A shop wants to send back stock they over-ordered. What do we charge?**
That is a commercial return, not a warranty claim. Restocking fee is 10 percent
of the invoice value, and only within 30 days of delivery. Goods have to come
back unmounted and in original packaging. If a headset has been pressed into a
frame it is not resaleable and we refuse it at the door.

**Custom paint?**
Never returnable. No exceptions, and do not ask Customer Operations to make one.

**How long does an RMA number stay open?**
14 days from issue. After that it expires and the shop has to request a new
one. Warehouse will not book in a carton without an RMA number written on the
outside.

## Warranty

**How long is the warranty on frames?**
24 months on aluminium, 36 months on carbon, from our invoice date to the
trade customer. Replacement units carry the remainder of the original period,
not a new one.

**Does the supplier pay for warranty replacements?**
Yes, wholesale value plus a EUR 12 handling fee per unit for suppliers on our
standard framework agreement terms. It only works if incoming inspection
recorded the lot number, which is why the lot number field is not optional.

## Lead times

**Where do I find lead times?**
The Delivery SLA table, by SKU prefix and supplier tier. Do not quote a lead
time from memory to a customer. Check the supplier's tier in its onboarding
record first, then read the row.

**Can we expedite?**
Yes, 8 percent surcharge on the order value, agreed case by case. Expediting
one order does not move any other order in the queue, and buyers should say so
explicitly when a customer asks for two.

## Quality

**A lot failed inspection. Who pays for sending it back?**
The supplier, or we scrap it at their cost when return freight would cost more
than the lot is worth. We do not rework supplier goods ourselves unless it is
agreed in writing with an agreed rate.

**How many rejects before something changes?**
Two rejected lots in six months and inspection goes to tightened severity.
Three and we can put the supplier on 100 percent inspection at their cost.

## Things people keep asking that this page cannot answer

- Freight insurance limits and who claims on the policy. Ask Finance.
- Customs classification codes for carbon frames. Ask the customs broker.
- Whether we can sell direct to consumers. We cannot, and that is a board-level
  decision, not a procurement one.
