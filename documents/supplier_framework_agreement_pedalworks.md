# Supplier Framework Agreement — Halden Cycle Supply B.V. and Pedalworks Manufacturing Ltd

Agreement reference: HCS-FA-2025-004
Effective date: 15 September 2025
Initial term: 18 months, renewing automatically for successive 12-month periods

## 1. Parties and Scope

This Framework Agreement is entered into between Halden Cycle Supply B.V., a
bicycle-parts distributor registered in Rotterdam ("the Distributor"), and
Pedalworks Manufacturing Ltd, a manufacturer of drivetrain components and
wheelsets registered in Taichung ("the Supplier").

This Agreement governs all purchase orders issued by the Distributor to the
Supplier. It does not itself commit the Distributor to any minimum volume. Each
purchase order is a separate contract of sale, incorporating these terms by
reference. Where a purchase order conflicts with this Agreement, this Agreement
prevails, except in respect of quantity, delivery address and requested
delivery date.

## 2. Product Scope

The Supplier is approved to deliver the following SKU families:

- DRV-CST-* — cassettes, 11 and 12 speed
- DRV-CHN-* — chains
- WHL-ALU-* — aluminium wheelsets, 700c and 650b

Any SKU outside these families requires a written scope extension signed by
both parties. Frame sets and carbon wheelsets are outside scope for the term of
this Agreement.

## 3. Pricing

3.1 Prices are those set out in Schedule A, expressed in USD, exclusive of any
duties.

3.2 Prices are fixed until 14 September 2026.

3.3 After the fixed period, the Supplier may request a price adjustment once
per calendar year, with 60 days' written notice, and only where the underlying
alloy or steel input index has moved by more than 8 percent since the last
adjustment.

3.4 The unit prices in Schedule A are 3 percent below the Supplier's list
price, agreed in exchange for the shortened payment terms in clause 4.1.

## 4. Payment Terms

4.1 Payment terms are net 30 days from the date of a valid invoice.

4.2 No early settlement discount is offered under this Agreement.

4.3 An invoice is valid only if it references a purchase order number, the
delivery note number, and the SKU codes as listed in Schedule A.

4.4 The minimum order value is USD 15,000 per purchase order. Orders below that
value are declined rather than surcharged.

## 5. Delivery Terms

5.1 Deliveries are made FCA Taichung (Incoterms 2020).

5.2 Lead times are governed by the Delivery SLA, document HCS-SLA-2024. Where
this Agreement and the Delivery SLA conflict on lead times or penalties, the
Delivery SLA prevails.

5.3 Title and risk pass to the Distributor on delivery to the nominated freight
forwarder in Taichung. Transit loss on the ocean leg is the Distributor's
exposure.

5.4 The Distributor arranges and pays ocean freight. A delay caused by freight
booking is not, of itself, a delay attributable to the Supplier under clause
5.2, unless the Supplier failed to present the goods to the forwarder on time.

## 6. Quality

6.1 The Supplier warrants that goods conform to the specifications in Schedule
A and to the Quality Inspection Procedure, document HCS-QIP-2023.

6.2 The first three lots of each SKU family are inspected at 100 percent before
normal sampling begins.

6.3 Repeated failure of incoming inspection — three rejected lots within any
rolling six-month period — entitles the Distributor to require 100 percent
inspection at the Supplier's cost.

## 7. Warranty and Returns

7.1 Warranty terms are set out in the Returns and Warranty Policy, document
HCS-RWP-2025.

7.2 The Supplier reimburses the Distributor for the wholesale value of any unit
replaced under warranty, plus a flat handling fee of USD 15 per unit.

## 8. Confidentiality

Each party keeps the other's commercial terms confidential for the term of this
Agreement and for 2 years afterwards.

## 9. Liability

9.1 Neither party's aggregate liability under this Agreement exceeds the total
value of purchase orders placed in the 6 months preceding the claim.

9.2 Clause 9.1 does not limit liability for death, personal injury, or the
Supplier's indemnity for product recall costs under clause 9.3.

9.3 Where a product recall is caused by a manufacturing defect, the Supplier
bears the full cost of the recall, including the Distributor's logistics and
customer communication costs.

## 10. Termination

10.1 Either party may terminate for convenience with 3 months' written notice,
effective no earlier than the end of the initial term.

10.2 Either party may terminate immediately for material breach not remedied
within 30 days of written notice.

10.3 Failure to meet the on-time delivery threshold in the Delivery SLA for
three consecutive quarters is a material breach for the purposes of clause 10.2.

## 11. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by Dutch law. The parties submit to the exclusive
jurisdiction of the courts of Rotterdam.
