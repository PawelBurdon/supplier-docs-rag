Halden Cycle Supply, and no audited forecast process. Tier 3 is the default for
probationary suppliers under the Delivery SLA, document HCS-SLA-2024. Lead

Promotion to Tier 2 is possible at the first review if the supplier meets the
Tier 3 OTIF target in three consecutive quarters and completes a successful
on-site audit.
