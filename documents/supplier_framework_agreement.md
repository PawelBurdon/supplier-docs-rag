# Supplier Framework Agreement — Halden Cycle Supply B.V. and Velocore Components GmbH

Agreement reference: HCS-FA-2024-011
Effective date: 1 March 2024
Initial term: 36 months, renewing automatically for successive 12-month periods

## 1. Parties and Scope

This Framework Agreement is entered into between Halden Cycle Supply B.V., a
bicycle-parts distributor registered in Rotterdam ("the Distributor"), and
Velocore Components GmbH, a manufacturer of aluminium and carbon frame sets
registered in Ingolstadt ("the Supplier").

This Agreement governs all purchase orders issued by the Distributor to the
Supplier. It does not itself commit the Distributor to any minimum volume. Each
purchase order is a separate contract of sale, incorporating these terms by
reference. Where a purchase order conflicts with this Agreement, this Agreement
prevails, except in respect of quantity, delivery address and requested
delivery date.

## 2. Product Scope

The Supplier is approved to deliver the following SKU families:

- FRM-ALU-* — aluminium frame sets, sizes 48 to 60
- FRM-CRB-* — carbon frame sets, sizes 48 to 58
- FRK-ALU-* — aluminium forks

Any SKU outside these families requires a written scope extension signed by
both parties. The Distributor's category buyer is not authorised to extend
scope by email.

## 3. Pricing

3.1 Prices are those set out in Schedule A, expressed in EUR, exclusive of VAT.

3.2 Prices are fixed for the first 12 months of the initial term.

3.3 After the fixed period, the Supplier may request a price adjustment once
per calendar year, with 90 days' written notice, and only where the underlying
aluminium or carbon input index has moved by more than 6 percent since the last
adjustment. The Distributor may reject an adjustment and terminate the affected
SKU family without penalty.

3.4 Tooling costs for new moulds are shared 50/50 and amortised over the first
2,000 units.

## 4. Payment Terms

4.1 Standard payment terms are net 45 days from the date of a valid invoice.

4.2 An early settlement discount of 2 percent applies to invoices paid within
10 days of receipt.

4.3 An invoice is valid only if it references a purchase order number, the
delivery note number, and the SKU codes as listed in Schedule A. Invoices
missing any of these are returned within 5 business days and the payment clock
restarts on resubmission.

4.4 The Distributor may set off any penalty payable under the Delivery SLA
(document HCS-SLA-2024) against amounts owed under this clause, provided the
penalty has been notified in writing within 30 days of the late delivery.

## 5. Delivery Terms

5.1 Deliveries are made DAP Rotterdam (Incoterms 2020) unless a purchase order
states otherwise.

5.2 Lead times are governed by the Delivery SLA, document HCS-SLA-2024, which
forms part of this Agreement. Where this Agreement and the Delivery SLA
conflict on lead times or penalties, the Delivery SLA prevails.

5.3 Title and risk pass to the Distributor on unloading at the Rotterdam
warehouse.

5.4 Partial deliveries are permitted only with prior written consent. An
unconsented partial delivery is treated as a late delivery of the whole order
for the purposes of clause 4.4.

## 6. Quality

6.1 The Supplier warrants that goods conform to the specifications in Schedule
A and to the Quality Inspection Procedure, document HCS-QIP-2023.

6.2 The Distributor performs incoming inspection on a sampling basis. Acceptance
of a sample does not waive any claim for latent defects.

6.3 Repeated failure of incoming inspection — three rejected lots within any
rolling six-month period — entitles the Distributor to require 100 percent
inspection at the Supplier's cost.

## 7. Warranty and Returns

7.1 Warranty terms are set out in the Returns and Warranty Policy, document
HCS-RWP-2025.

7.2 The Supplier reimburses the Distributor for the wholesale value of any unit
replaced under warranty, plus a flat handling fee of EUR 12 per unit.

## 8. Confidentiality

Each party keeps the other's commercial terms confidential for the term of this
Agreement and for 3 years afterwards. Aggregate, anonymised performance data may
be shared with the Distributor's other suppliers for benchmarking.

## 9. Liability

9.1 Neither party's aggregate liability under this Agreement exceeds the total
value of purchase orders placed in the 12 months preceding the claim.

9.2 Clause 9.1 does not limit liability for death, personal injury, or the
Supplier's indemnity for product recall costs under clause 9.3.

9.3 Where a product recall is caused by a manufacturing defect, the Supplier
bears the full cost of the recall, including the Distributor's logistics and
customer communication costs.

## 10. Termination

10.1 Either party may terminate for convenience with 6 months' written notice,
effective no earlier than the end of the initial term.

10.2 Either party may terminate immediately for material breach not remedied
within 30 days of written notice.

10.3 Failure to meet the on-time delivery threshold in the Delivery SLA for
three consecutive quarters is a material breach for the purposes of clause 10.2.

## 11. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by Dutch law. The parties submit to the exclusive
jurisdiction of the courts of Rotterdam.
